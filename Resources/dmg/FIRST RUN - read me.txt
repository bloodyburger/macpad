MacPad — development build

This build is signed ad-hoc but NOT notarized by Apple, so macOS will refuse
to open it on the first try. That is Gatekeeper doing its job on an unsigned
download, not a broken app.

To open it:

  1. Drag MacPad.app to your Applications folder.
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) MacPad.app and choose "Open".
  3. Click "Open" in the dialog that appears.

Only the first launch needs this. If step 2 is refused, run this in Terminal:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacPad.app

What to expect in this build: tabs, syntax highlighting, find and replace
with Perl-syntax regular expressions, encodings, line endings, and sessions
that come back after every launch. See docs/FEATURES.md in the repository
for what is and is not done.
