MacPad 0.1.0 is the first public build: a native text and code editor for macOS 13 and later, built on Scintilla 5.6.9, Lexilla 5.5.5 and Boost.Regex 1.90 inside a Swift and AppKit app.

**In this build**

- Tabs you can drag to reorder, close and reopen; double-click the empty bar for a new one.
- Syntax highlighting for 30 languages, with folding, line numbers, bookmarks and indent guides.
- Find and Replace in Normal, Extended and Regular-expression modes, with full Perl-syntax regular expressions; matches highlight as you type; Count, Mark All, Replace All.
- Open files from the Finder, the File menu or by dragging them onto the window.
- UTF-8, UTF-16 and 8-bit encodings; Windows, Unix and classic Mac line endings, detected and convertible.
- Session restore: your tabs come back on the next launch, and unsaved text is backed up every 7 seconds, so quitting never loses work.
- Light and dark themes that follow macOS.
- Check for Updates, from the MacPad menu and once a day at launch.

**Installing**

Open the disk image and drag MacPad to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it cannot verify the app, because the build is signed but not notarised by Apple. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about MacPad and click Open Anyway. On macOS 13 and 14 you can right-click MacPad and choose Open instead. Only the first launch needs this.

**Known limits**

Preferences, Find in Files, split view, macros and plugins are not in this build. `docs/FEATURES.md` lists every feature and its state.

Universal binary for Apple silicon and Intel. Free software under the GNU GPL v3 or later.
