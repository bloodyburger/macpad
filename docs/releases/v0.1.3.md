MacPad 0.1.3 fixes a tab jumping to the end of its text, and corrects the install instructions for recent macOS.

**Fixed**

- Coming back to a tab no longer scrolls it to the end. A tab now stays exactly where you left it, up and down and sideways, when you switch tabs and when MacPad backs up your unsaved text in the background. Before, the view jumped to the caret, which after typing or pasting sits at the end of the text.
- The scroll position of each tab, including sideways scroll on long lines, is now remembered between launches.

**Changed**

- The disk image is now named after its version, such as "MacPad 0.1.3", so several mounted at once can be told apart. Eject older MacPad images before installing a new one.
- The first-launch instructions now describe macOS 15 and later, where right-click then Open no longer works.

**Installing**

Open the disk image and drag MacPad to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it cannot verify the app, because the build is signed but not notarised by Apple. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about MacPad and click Open Anyway. On macOS 13 and 14 you can right-click MacPad and choose Open instead. Only the first launch needs this.
