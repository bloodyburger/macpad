MacPad 0.1.4 helps with installing: it notices when it is running from its disk image, and it tidies up old images.

**New**

- Opened straight from the disk image, MacPad now says so, shows its version, and offers to move itself to your Applications folder. An older MacPad already there goes to the Trash, and your open tabs carry over.
- Once MacPad runs from Applications, it ejects any MacPad disk images that are still mounted, so an old copy cannot be opened by mistake.

MacPad does not delete the downloaded `.dmg` files in your Downloads folder; those are yours to remove.

**Installing**

Open the disk image and drag MacPad to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it cannot verify the app, because the build is signed but not notarised by Apple. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about MacPad and click Open Anyway. On macOS 13 and 14 you can right-click MacPad and choose Open instead. Only the first launch needs this.
